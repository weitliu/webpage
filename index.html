THE PROBLEMS WE ADDRESS

From manual entry
to clearer business decisions.

  1. Invoice entry takes too much time

    Staff spend too much time manually entering supplier invoices.

  2. Purchasing changes are easy to miss

    Price changes, promotions, case-pack differences, and new products are easy to overlook and are handled inconsistently.

  3. Sales totals do not tell the whole story

    Managers can see total sales, but may not see which SKUs actually make money, which products tie up cash, or which are about to run out.

  4. Replenishment is reactive

    Fast-selling products can run out while slow-moving stock piles up.

  5. Knowledge stays with a few people

    Purchasing and pricing expertise is held by a small number of people, making it difficult to turn that knowledge into a lasting capability for the store.
